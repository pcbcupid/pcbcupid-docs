The Glyph RP2040 is designed for easy hand assembly with standard through-hole headers. The 0.1" pitch pads are spaced to accept male or female header strips, or you can solder wires directly for a permanent connection.

### Header Installation

1. **Choose your headers** — For breadboard use, solder male headers pointing down. For mounting the Glyph as a daughterboard, use female headers or right-angle headers depending on your enclosure.
2. **Position the headers** — Insert the header pins through the pads from the top side of the board. The long pins should extend through the bottom. For a flush mount, place the plastic header body against the top surface.
3. **Secure the board** — Place the board on a flat surface with the header pins pointing down. Use a breadboard or a piece of foam to hold the pins perpendicular while you solder.
4. **Solder the pins** — Apply solder to each pad, ensuring the joint flows completely around the pin and fills the pad. Use a fine-tip iron at 350°C (660°F) and leaded solder for best results.
5. **Trim excess** — After soldering, clip any excess pin length from the bottom side with flush cutters.
6. **Inspect** — Check each joint for cold solder or bridges. Use a magnifier if needed; the 2.54 mm pitch gives plenty of room between pins.

### Direct Wire Soldering

For permanent installations, you can solder wires directly to the pads. Strip 3–4 mm of insulation, tin the wire, and solder to the pad. Use heat-shrink tubing over each joint to prevent shorts. This method is ideal for embedding the board in a project enclosure.

### Board-to-Board Mounting

The through-hole pads can also serve as castellated connections for stacking the Glyph directly onto a carrier PCB. Apply solder paste to the carrier's matching pads, place the Glyph on top, and reflow or hand-solder each joint. Ensure the board is flat and aligned before soldering to avoid skewed connections.
