|-----|-------|----------|-------|
| 1 | RESET | Reset | Active-low, pulls RP2040 into reset |
| 2, 3 | +3.3V | Power | Regulated 3.3V output |
| 4, 16 | GND | Ground | Common ground reference |
| 5 | GPIO00 | Digital I/O | Also I2C0 SDA, UART0 TX, SPI0 RX |
| 6 | GPIO01 | Digital I/O | Also I2C0 SCL, UART0 RX, SPI0 CS |
| 7 | GPIO02 | Digital I/O | Also SPI0 SCK, I2C1 SDA |
| 8 | GPIO03 | Digital I/O | Also SPI0 TX, I2C1 SCL |
| 9 | GPIO08 | Digital I/O | Also UART1 TX, SPI1 RX |
| 10 | GPIO05 | Digital I/O | Also SPI0 CS, UART1 RX |
| 11 | GPIO06 | Digital I/O | Also SPI0 SCK, UART1 TX |
| 12 | GPIO07 | Digital I/O | Also SPI0 TX, UART1 RX |
| 13 | GPIO04 | Digital I/O | Also SPI0 RX, I2C0 SDA |
| 14 | GPIO13 | Digital I/O | Also UART1 TX, I2C1 SCL |
| 15 | GPIO12 | Digital I/O | Also UART1 RX, I2C1 SDA |
| 17 | +BATT | Power | Direct battery connection (3.7–4.2V) |
| 18 | L_EN | Enable | Regulator enable, active-high |
| 19 | VBUS | Power | USB 5V input |
| 20 | GPIO23 | Digital I/O | Also SPI1 SCK |
| 21 | GPIO24 | Digital I/O | Also SWD CLK, SPI1 TX |
| 22 | GPIO25 | Digital I/O | Also SWD IO, SPI1 RX, onboard LED |
| 23 | GPIO26 | Analog | ADC0, also I2C1 SCL |
| 24 | GPIO27 | Analog | ADC1, also I2C1 SDA |
| 25 | GPIO28 | Analog | ADC2, also SPI1 CS |
| 26 | GPIO29 | Digital I/O | Also ADC3 (unusable as ADC on RP2040) |
| 27 | GPIO20 | Digital I/O | Also UART0 TX, SPI0 RX |
| 28 | GPIO21 | Digital I/O | Also UART0 RX, SPI0 TX |
