Introducing the **Glyph S3 Motor Driver**, a powerful microcontroller and motor drive development board combining the ESP32-S3 chip with an onboard **Toshiba TB6612FNG dual H-bridge motor driver**. It controls two brushed DC motors independently or one bipolar stepper motor, while the dual-core S3 handles compute, sensing, motion algorithms, and wireless IoT connectivity in a single compact footprint.

- **Dimensions**: Measures 50.9mm × 22.9mm × 1.6mm (without headers soldered)
- **Processor**: ESP32-S3 dual-core LX7 microprocessor @ 240 MHz, 3.3V logic and power
- **Memory**:
  - **SRAM**: 512 KB Internal SRAM
  - **Flash Memory**: 8 MB Quad SPI Flash
  - **PSRAM**: None
- **GPIO**: Rich set of GPIO pins with the following capabilities:
  - 9 GPIO pins support 12-bit ADC (ADC1): IO1 (BATT_MSR), IO2, IO4, IO5, IO6, IO7, IO8, IO9, IO10
  - I2C (SDA IO4, SCL IO5)
  - SPI (SCK IO35, MOSI IO36, MISO IO37)
  - UART (uTx IO43, uRx IO44)
  - Native USB Serial / JTAG
- **Integrated Motor Driver (TB6612FNG)**:
  - Dual independent H-bridge channels on 2-pin Molex PicoBlade connectors
  - Supports 2 brushed DC motors (CW, CCW, short brake, stop) or 1 bipolar 4-wire stepper motor
  - 1.2A average continuous output current per channel (3.2A peak pulse)
  - Dedicated direction, PWM speed, and standby (STBY) control pins wired to ESP32-S3 GPIOs
- **Power Management**:
  - USB Type-C connector for power, programming, and serial port debugging
  - 3.3 low-dropout voltage regulator rated for up to 900mA continuous load
  - Built-in MCP73831 single-cell LiPo/Li-ion charger with charging status indicator LED
  - Automatic hot-swap power-path between Battery (+BATT) and USB (VBUS)
  - Battery voltage monitoring on D1 / BATT_MSR (GPIO1)
  - Reverse voltage protection diode and polyfuse
- **Additional Features**:
  - Onboard user LED (connected to GPIO21)
  - Onboard GLINK connector for easy integration with Qwiic and STEMMA QT I2C sensors & modules
  - High-quality tactile Reset and Bootloader buttons for easy access

### Integrated Dual Motor Control

The onboard Toshiba TB6612FNG provides high-efficiency MOSFET-based H-bridge outputs with lower heat dissipation compared to traditional bipolar drivers like the L298N. It supports flexible control modes including forward, reverse, brake, coast, and standby.

- **Motor Supply (VM) Range:** 2.5V – 13.5V
- **Output Current:** 1.2A average continuous per channel; 3.2A single-pulse peak
- **Control Signals:** Direction pins (AIN1/2, BIN1/2), speed pins (PWMA, PWMB), and standby (STBY)

<Warning>
Always ensure motor stall current stays within safe operating parameters and check polarity before powering. Do not treat the 3.2A single-pulse peak rating as continuous current. See [Toshiba's TB6612FNG datasheet](https://toshiba.semicon-storage.com/info/datasheet_en_20141001.pdf?did=10660).
</Warning>

### Native Bootloader and Programming

The Glyph S3 Motor Driver features a built-in USB bootloader that makes programming new firmware easy and keeps the cost of the board down. On first time use, hold down the **BOOT** button while plugging it into USB, and it will appear as a COM port in your Device Manager. If you face any issue related to hardware not detecting on your system, please check out our troubleshooting guide [here](https://learn.pcbcupid.com/boards/needs/arduino-ide-setup#hardware-not-detecting).

### Project Applications

The integration of dual H-bridge motor drivers with an ESP32-S3 makes this board ideal for:
- Two-wheel differential-drive mobile robots
- Wi-Fi/Bluetooth remote-controlled rovers and cars
- Pan-and-tilt motorized camera mounts
- Automated smart blinds and motorized valves
- Compact robotic arms and stepper-driven linear slides
